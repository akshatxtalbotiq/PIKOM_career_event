# PIKOM Project Vision

PIKOM is envisioned as a **complete digital platform for managing and experiencing a career festival** — covering both the organizer side and the attendee experience.

### 1. Event Management

Organizers should be able to create and configure the entire event from one platform:

- Event details, dates, venue and branding
- Registration and attendee management
- Stage and session planning
- Floor map and booth setup
- Interview scheduling rules
- Event communications and check-in

```text
                 PIKOM EVENT
                     │
        ┌────────────┼────────────┐
        ↓            ↓            ↓
     Sessions      Floor Map    Registration
        │            │            │
      Agenda       Booths       Attendees
        │            │            │
        └────────────┼────────────┘
                     ↓
              Event Management
```

### 2. Attendee Experience

Once registered, attendees get a single place to explore and plan their festival experience.

```text
                  ATTENDEE
                     │
              Event Dashboard
                     │
       ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
       ↓             ↓             ↓
   Employers      Sessions      Floor Map
   & Jobs          & Agenda       & Booths
       │             │             │
       ↓             ↓             ↓
   Interviews     Register       Navigate
       │             │             │
       └─────────────┼─────────────┘
                     ↓
                My Schedule
```

### 3. Career & Networking

The platform can bring different parts of the career festival together:

- **Employers** → companies, jobs and interview opportunities
- **Universities** → institutions and programs
- **Training Providers** → courses, promotions and vouchers
- **Stage Sessions** → panels, presentations and live sessions
- **Booths** → physical locations that attendees can discover through the floor map

### 4. Complete Event Journey

The overall experience should connect the digital and physical sides of the event:

```text
Create Event
     ↓
Configure Event
     ↓
Publish
     ↓
Attendee Registers
     ↓
Explore Employers / Universities / Training
     ↓
Book Interviews + Sessions
     ↓
View Personal Schedule
     ↓
Use Floor Map → Find Booths
     ↓
Attend Event + Check In
```

### End Goal

PIKOM should become more than a **registration system**. It should act as the **digital layer of the entire career festival** — helping organizers manage the event while giving attendees one place to discover opportunities, plan their schedule, book activities, and navigate the physical event.
