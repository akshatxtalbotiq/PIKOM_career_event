Before you begin

  • Sign in with your administrator account. Every screen in this manual requires you to be signed in.
  • The left-hand menu holds Campaigns, Registration Forms, Post-event Surveys, Golf Events, Team & Access, User Manual (this page — it opens in its own tab, so you can keep it beside the screen you are working on) and, at the bottom, Log out.
  • Permissions. Superusers see everything. Everyone else only sees campaigns whose Campaign team they belong to, and can only build forms and read registrations for those campaigns. If a page returns a permission error, ask a superuser to add you to the campaign team.
Two steps, always in this order. A campaign comes first, then the registration form is attached to it. Creating the form first and attaching it later works, but you lose the event details and email wording that are driven by the campaign until the link is made.

Part A — Create a campaign

A1Open the Campaigns list

Click Campaigns in the left menu. The cards at the top count your campaigns, how many are live or upcoming, and the registrations across all forms. Each row below is one campaign, with its dates, an Active or Inactive badge, its timing (Live, Upcoming or Ended) and a set of action buttons on the right. Use the All / Live / Upcoming / Ended filter, Search campaigns and Show … per page to narrow a long list.

A2Click + New Campaign

The New campaign dialog opens on top of the list, grouped into Basics, Schedule, Team, Theme colour and Check-in. Nothing is saved until you press Save campaign, so you can back out at any point with Cancel.

A3Fill in the campaign details
Field What to enter
Campaign titleRequired. Appears in the campaign list, on the dashboard and in registrant emails. If one event has more than one form, name them distinctly — e.g. … - REGISTRATION and … - SPONSORSHIP.
Campaign is activeOn = the campaign is live and shows the green Active badge. Turn it off to retire a campaign without deleting it; inactive campaigns stop accepting registrations.
StartsClick the field to open the calendar, choose the day, then set the hour and minutes on the bottom row of the picker. Defaults to 12:00 AM.
EndsSame picker, defaults to 11:59 PM. This date is reused as the event date in the confirmation and reminder emails, so set it to the actual event day.
Reply-to emailWhere replies from registrants go. More than one address is allowed — separate them with commas, no spaces.
Campaign teamThe users allowed to see and manage this campaign and its forms. Click the box and pick from the list; repeat to add several people.
Theme colourAccent colour used on the public form and in registrant emails. Click one of the preset swatches, or Custom to pick any colour. The value must be a 6-digit hex code; anything else falls back to the system default.
QR scanning pageTurns on the QR check-in scanner for this campaign. Leave off unless you will scan QR codes at the door.
Show check-in detailsOn = when a QR code is scanned, staff see the registrant’s details. Leave on for events you will check people in at.
A4Check the dialog, then Save

Compare your entries against the example below, then press Save campaign. The dialog closes and the new campaign appears in the list.

A5Know the row buttons

Once the campaign exists, its row carries the round buttons below. Hover over one to see its name. Which ones appear depends on whether a registration form is attached yet.

Icon Action
Bar chartDashboard — headline numbers for the campaign.
PeopleRegistrants — the registrations list for the attached form.
PencilEdit campaign — reopens the same dialog you used to create the campaign.
… (more)Opens the form actions: Edit registration form (Form Builder, Part C), Open public form (the live page, in a new tab), Copy registration link (copies the public URL to your clipboard) and Open check-in scanner (the on-site scanning page for phones and Sunmi devices).
Plus boxShown instead of the … button when no form exists yet: Create registration form. It jumps straight to Part B with this campaign pre-selected. The row also says No registration form · Create one under the title.
ClockLegacy entry form, for older campaigns that still use one.
A6The campaign dashboard

The bar-chart button opens a live summary: total registrations, how many are approved, how many checked in, the no-show count, the approval funnel with emails sent, registrations over time, top organisations and approval status. Print report saves it as a PDF.

Part B — Create a registration form

B1Open Registration Forms

Click Registration Forms in the left menu — or click the plus-box button on a campaign row, which opens this page with that campaign already selected for you. With three forms or fewer, each form shows as a card with its registration count; with more, they are listed in a table.

B2Click + New Registration Form and fill it in

The New registration form dialog opens. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Field What to enter
Form titleRequired. Shown as the heading of the public page and in the forms list.
CampaignThe campaign this form belongs to. Pick it here: the event details and email wording in Form Builder depend on it. The list only offers campaigns you are allowed to manage.
Opens / ClosesThe window in which the public form accepts entries. Outside it, visitors see the “registration closed” wording.
Public URL (optional)The readable part of the public address. Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only. Leave it blank and one is generated from the title. It must be unique — if another form already uses it, saving fails with a message telling you so.
DescriptionRich text shown at the top of the public form — venue, closing date, who to contact. The small toolbar handles bold, headings, lists and links.
Form is activeLeave on so the form can be opened. Inactive forms stop accepting new responses.
Where is the real link? Don’t hand-build the public URL from this dialog. After saving, open Form Builder — the complete, correct link is displayed at the top under Shareable link, with a Copy button.
B3Save — five fields are created for you

When you save a new registration form, the platform seeds the core registrant fields so the form works end to end immediately:

Field Required by default
NameYes
Participant TypeNo — a fixed picklist (Delegate, Speaker, Sponsor, Organizer …)
EmailYes
PhoneNo
OrganizationNo

These are identity fields. They feed the registrations list, the QR confirmation and the check-in screen, so they cannot be deleted — you can only make them optional or reword them.

B4Actions on the forms list

Every form has the same set of actions. On a card they are labelled buttons along the bottom; in the table view the same actions are round icon buttons at the right-hand end of the row.

Icon Action
Details (pencil)Edit details — reopens the dialog from Step B2.
Questions (sliders)Build questions — opens Form Builder (Part C).
Preview (eye)Preview — see the form as a registrant would; nothing is saved.
… → Open public formThe live page, in a new tab.
… → Copy public linkCopies the public URL to your clipboard.
… → Open check-in scannerThe scanning page for the event day.
… → Consolidated reportA report of all the responses.
… → Clone to another campaignCopy its questions into another campaign. The fastest way to run this year’s event off last year’s form (see section 6).
… → DeleteIrreversible — check the registration count first.

Part C — Set up the form in Form Builder

Form Builder opens with the form’s Shareable link at the top, and a View form button beside the title. Below the link, Form content has four tabs: Questions, Banner, Event details and Emails.

Shareable link and banner

Copy puts the public link on your clipboard. View form opens a small menu: Preview shows the form without saving anything, Open live form opens the real page. The green Accepting responses label shows the form is open. On the Banner tab, upload the image shown at the top of the public form (and, if you choose, the emails): PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 5 MB. Use Upload banner or Choose an image; once one is set you can replace or remove it.

Event details

On the Event details tab. These lines appear in the confirmation and reminder emails, and — if you switch the toggle on — beside the public form as well.

DateRead-only. Taken from the campaign’s end date. To change it, edit the campaign.
TimeFree text, e.g. 10.00 am – 1.00 pm.
VenueFree text, multiple lines allowed.
Dress codeFree text.
Additional info rowsExtra lines such as parking, agenda or lucky draw. Each row takes an emoji, an optional bold label, the text, and an optional link — when a link is given the text becomes clickable. + Add row adds more, × removes one.
Show event details on this registration formPer-form toggle. On = the block above is also shown at the top of the public page.

Press Save event details when done — this block has its own save button. Event details apply to the whole campaign, so every form on that campaign shares them.

Email content

On the Emails tab. Four tabs, one per message: Thank-you (sent on submission), QR confirmation, Attendance reminder and Registration closed. For each you can write a subject line and the body.

  • The small tag buttons — [Name], [Reg no], [Date], [Event] — drop in a placeholder that is filled per recipient. There is one set for the subject and one for the message.
  • Leave the subject blank to use the system default.
  • Two switches per message: show the banner image at the top, and include the event details block.
  • Sign-off is the closing block — several lines allowed, with bold and colour.
  • Press Save email content, and use Preview to see the rendered email.
Form fields

The Questions tab is the question list (the number beside the tab name is how many there are). Each row shows its number, the question text, its type, a Required or Optional tag, and a List column tag when the answer is shown as a column in the registrations list.

  • Drag the handle on the left to reorder; numbering updates itself.
  • Pencil edits a question, bin deletes it.
  • Identity fields (padlock) have no bin — mark them optional instead of removing them.
Add a question

Click + Add question at the top right of the Questions tab.

Field What to enter
Question textRequired. Basic HTML is accepted if you need a link or bold inside the label.
Help textOptional one-liner shown under the question.
Answer typeShort text · Long text (paragraph) · Dropdown · Single choice (radio) · Multiple choice (checkbox).
RequiredOn = the registrant cannot submit without answering.
Show in registrations listOn = the answer becomes a column in the registrations list. Use it for the handful of answers your team sorts and filters on, not for everything.
Column headerShort label for that column, up to 60 characters. Blank = the question text, truncated. Only used when Show in registrations list is on.
ChoicesShown for Dropdown, Single choice and Multiple choice. One row per option; + Add choice adds more, the drag handle or the up/down arrows reorder, × removes. Add follow-up attaches a free-text box to a specific option — useful for “Other, please specify”.
Include an “Other” free-text answerAdds a catch-all “Other” option with its own text box.
Max selectionsMultiple choice only. Caps how many boxes a registrant may tick; blank = no limit.

Press Save question. The question appears at the bottom of the list — drag it into position. Cancel discards it.

Clone a form for a repeat event

When you run the same event again, don’t rebuild the form. Clone last year’s and point the copy at this year’s campaign — every question comes across with its type, choices, required flags and Show in list settings. Registrations do not.

1Choose Clone to another campaign

Open Registration Forms, find the form you want to reuse, click its … button and choose Clone to another campaign — just above Delete.

2Name the copy and choose its campaign
Field What to enter
New form titleArrives pre-filled as Copy of …. Replace it with the real name for this year’s event — the copy’s public URL is generated from this title, so a clean title gives a clean link.
Assign to campaignThe campaign the copy belongs to — normally the new campaign you created for this year. Only campaigns you are allowed to manage are listed.

Press Clone form. The list reloads with the new form on it, ready to edit. Cancel leaves everything untouched.

3Fix the three things a copy can’t know
  1. The dates. Survey start and end are copied from the original, so they are still last year’s window. Click Details on the new form and set the new one.
  2. The public URL. It is generated from the new title. Open Form Builder and check the link under Shareable link before you share it.
  3. Event details and email wording. These belong to the campaign, not the form, so the copy shows whatever the new campaign holds — usually empty. Fill them in from Form Builder (Part C).
What a clone does and does not carry over
Copied Not copied
Every question — text, help text, type, choices, follow-up boxes, “Other”, max selections, Required, Show in list and its column header · the identity field setup · the form description · start and end dates · active status · the banner image, as its own separate file, so replacing it on one form never touches the other. Registrations and their answers · QR and check-in state · the campaign’s event details and email wording, which live on the campaign rather than the form.
Also useful for testing. To try a change without touching a form that is already collecting registrations, clone it, experiment on the copy, then delete the copy.

Test and go live

  1. In Form Builder, click View form → Preview to check the page without writing any data.
  2. Then View form → Open live form and submit one real test entry yourself, so you can confirm the thank-you page and the confirmation email.
  3. Check the entry landed in the registrations list, then delete it before you publish the link.
  4. Share the link from Copy — not a URL you typed by hand.
Where registrations arrive

Campaigns → people button (or click the form’s title on Registration Forms). Each row is one registrant, with the registration number, approval status, check-in and QR state. The Overview cards summarise Total registrants, Pending, Approved, Rejected, QR sent, Reminders and Checked in. The public link and Register walk-in sit at the top; from the toolbar you can edit an entry, change status or participant type, and send QR codes or reminders. Tick rows to act on several people at once.

Pre-launch checklist

  • Campaign end date is the real event date — the emails read it from there.
  • The reply-to email is a mailbox someone actually watches.
  • Everyone who needs access is on the campaign team.
  • The form is attached to the right campaign, and its start/end window is correct.
  • Banner uploaded; event details saved; the four emails read the way you want.
  • Required flags are right, and the answers your team works from have Show in list on.
  • One test registration submitted, checked and removed.
  • Campaign is Active and the form is Active.

Troubleshooting

Symptom Likely cause
“This URL is already used by another form.”The Public URL you typed is taken. Choose another, or leave it blank to auto-generate.
Permission error opening Form BuilderYou are not a superuser and not on that campaign’s team. Ask to be added.
The campaign does not appear in the Campaign list of the form dialogSame reason — the dropdown only lists campaigns you may manage.
Wrong date in the emailsThe date comes from the campaign’s end date. Edit the campaign, not the form.
Event details missing from the public pageShow event details on this registration form is off, or Save event details was not pressed.
Registrants see “registration closed”Today is outside the form’s start/end window, or the form or campaign is inactive.
Theme colour ignoredThe value was not a valid 6-digit hex code, so the default was used.
An identity field has no delete buttonBy design. Make it optional instead.
“Permission denied” when cloningYou need rights over both ends: the form you are copying and the campaign you are copying it into.
A cloned form shows last year’s datesExpected — dates are copied with the questions. Edit the new form and set its own window.
